How each number is calculated
Source
The "Blindbox History Report S2" spreadsheet. Download it with File › Download › Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) and load it here. A CSV of the "BlindBox History Report" tab also works, with fewer sections.
Box claims
Rows in the BlindBox History Report tab, deduplicated by reference_no and dated by created_time. All statuses count as a claim.
Failure rate
Claims with status FAILED divided by all claims that day.
Claiming users
Distinct customer_id with at least one claim that day. New users opened their first box that day; everyone else is returning.
Total participants
Distinct customer_id with any claim up to and including the report day.
Milestone reached
The first day a customer opened the box tied to a stamp milestone (stamp_milestone_at_open). Users who reached a milestone but have not opened its box are not visible in this export.
Highest milestone
Each user is placed in one segment: the highest milestone box they have opened by the report day.
Cashback paid
Sum of cashback_amount on successful CASHBACK claims.
Days of cover
Remaining Reward from the Stock Monitoring tab divided by the average successful payouts of that reward over the 7 days before the report day.
Coupon gap
Successful COUPON claims minus rows in Coupon Receive Report with a blindbox act_id (B13, B14…), by transaction day. Manual injections are rows from the Manual Inject tab.
Partial day
If the newest day in the file ends before 23:00, it is marked partial and the report day defaults to the day before.
Alert thresholds
Failure rate above 2% (warning) or 5% (critical); claims more than 30% below the 7-day average; any reward with under 14 days (warning) or 7 days (critical) of cover; coupon gap above 5% of coupons won.
